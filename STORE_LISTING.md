### Full Description (3,625 / 4,000 characters)
```text
Turn your Android smartphone into a high-performance, studio-grade PC webcam. Stream up to 1080p 60 FPS video directly to Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, OBS Studio, and Discord with ultra-low latency over USB or local Wi-Fi.

Unlike standard webcam apps, Otter is built on a 100% Peer-to-Peer (P2P), Zero-Cloud architecture. Your video frames stay entirely on your local cable or private local network. Zero telemetry, zero accounts, and zero cloud relays.

KEY FEATURES

- High-Fidelity Video: Hardware-accelerated H.264 video streaming with dynamic resolution detection: 4K UHD, 1080p FHD, 720p HD, and 480p SD.
- High Frame Rate: Smooth 60 FPS, 30 FPS, and 15 FPS capture.
- Ultra-Low Latency USB Streaming: Plug and play over USB via automatic ADB tunneling with near-zero latency and no root required.
- Wireless Wi-Fi Streaming: Connect over local Wi-Fi with automatic desktop client IP detection.
- Pro Manual Camera Controls:
  - ISO: Auto, 100, 200, 400, 800, 1600, 3200.
  - Shutter Speed: Auto, 1/2000s down to 1/15s.
  - Exposure Compensation: Dedicated EV - / EV + steppers and fine slider (-4.0 EV to +4.0 EV).
  - Focus: Toggle continuous Auto Focus or use the precision Manual Focus distance slider from Macro (0.1m) to Infinity.
  - White Balance: Auto, Daylight, Cloudy, Fluorescent, Incandescent/Tungsten, Twilight, and Shade.
  - Creative Effects: Normal Color, B&W Mono, Negative, Sepia, Solarize, and Posterize.
- Dual Virtual Background Engine:
  - Offload to PC GPU/CPU (Recommended): Keeps your phone cool and preserves battery while your computer handles real-time background blur, custom images, and chroma keying.
  - Mobile Processing: Standalone processing directly on your phone.
  - Background Modes: Custom background image, light portrait bokeh blur, heavy privacy blur, and green screen chroma keying.
- Display & Battery Optimization:
  - Keep Screen On: User-selectable toggle to keep display awake or allow device auto-sleep.
  - Dim Display Mode: Minimizes screen backlight brightness by up to 70% during long calls to prevent overheating and save battery.
  - Foreground Service: Background lock prevents Android OEM battery managers from interrupting active streams.
- Lighting & Framing:
  - Hardware Flashlight: Low and high-strength torch control for rear cameras.
  - Front Screen Glow Ring: Warm amber, daylight, sunset, and clean white illumination for front selfie cameras.
  - 360-Degree Rotation: 0, 90, 180, and 270 degree rotation with aspect-ratio preserving pillarboxing for custom or inverted mounts.
  - Horizontal Mirroring: Instant flip toggle for natural framing.
- Full-Resolution Ultra Photo: Capture uncompressed still photos at your camera sensor maximum hardware resolution (e.g. 64MP+) without interrupting the live stream.
- Quick Modes: One-tap presets for Meetings, Online Class, Live Streaming, Document Scan/OCR, Security Monitor, and Pro Manual.

HOW TO USE

1. USB Mode (Recommended):
   - Enable USB Debugging in Android Developer Options.
   - Connect phone to PC with a USB cable and grant debugging permission.
   - Launch Otter Desktop on Windows, macOS, or Linux, select USB mode, and click Connect & Start.

2. Wi-Fi Mode:
   - Ensure phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
   - Enter the IP address shown in the mobile app into the desktop client.
   - Click Connect & Start.

PRIVACY & OPEN SOURCE

- 100% Peer-to-Peer: No video or audio is ever uploaded to developer or third-party servers.
- Open Source: Released under the permissive MIT license.
- Developer: spacecar
- Official GitHub: https://github.com/saprecar/Otter
```
