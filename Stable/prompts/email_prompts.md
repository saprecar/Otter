# Business & Cold Outreach Email Prompts

Minimalist, high-conversion email prompts designed to strip conversational slop.

---

### 1. High-Impact B2B Outreach Email
```markdown
Role: Executive Communication Specialist.
Task: Draft a 4-sentence cold outreach email pitching technical services to VP/Director.
Structure:
- Line 1: Relevant observation about their product/stack.
- Line 2: Concrete value proposition with quantifiable metric (e.g. 30% latency cut).
- Line 3: Proof point (case study reference without exaggeration).
- Line 4: Low-friction call to action (10-minute discovery call).
Constraints:
- Under 80 words total.
- No "I hope this email finds you well" or "In today's fast-paced world".
```

---

### 2. Technical Status & Incident Notification
```markdown
Role: Engineering Operations Manager.
Task: Write an internal incident update email for engineering and leadership.
Format:
- Subject: [INCIDENT-STATUS] [Service Name] - [Resolved/Investigating]
- Impact: 1-line affected user count/percentage.
- Root Cause: 2 sentences technical root cause.
- Mitigation & Next Steps: 2 bullet points.
Constraints: Strictly factual; no apologies or speculative language.
```
